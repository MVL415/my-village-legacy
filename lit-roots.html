📚 Book of the Month

The Astral Library

by Kate Quinn

This month’s selection celebrates the magic of books and the worlds they create for us. Kate Quinn takes readers into the Astral Library, a hidden sanctuary where books become doorways to new places, new lives, and new possibilities. Through Alix’s journey, the story explores the power of imagination, the comfort we find in stories, and the courage it takes to step beyond the life we know. It’s a love letter to libraries, librarians, and every reader who has ever wished they could disappear into the pages of a favorite book.

Discussion Prompt:

What role have libraries and books played in your life? What can we do to make sure they remain a place of refuge, learning, and imagination for future generations?
